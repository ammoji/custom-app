# Privacy Policy — HamaraSetu

**Effective date:** May 16, 2026
**Last updated:** May 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HamaraSetu ("we", "our", "us")
collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use
our mobile application ("the App").

If you have any questions, contact us at: **sarastacklabs@gmail.com**

---

## 1. Who we are

HamaraSetu is a mobile application that connects customers with
local kirana (grocery) shops in India, with optional delivery via
independent delivery partners. The App is currently operated by
Sara Stack Labs. HamaraSetu is in beta and not yet available to the
general public.

---

## 2. Information we collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the App's
core functionality.

### 2.1 Account and profile information

When you create an account or use the App, we collect:

- **Phone number** — required for sign-in via OTP (one-time password).
  This is your account identifier.
- **Name** (optional) — used on order receipts and to address you in
  the App.
- **Email address** (optional) — used to send order receipts and
  important account notifications.

### 2.2 Address information

When you place an order or save an address for future use, we
collect:

- **Recipient name, phone, and delivery address** (line 1, line 2,
  city, pincode) — used to deliver your order.

You may save multiple addresses in your profile. You may delete any
saved address at any time from the Profile screen.

### 2.3 Order information

We collect and retain:

- **Order contents** (items, quantities, prices)
- **Payment method** (Cash on Delivery or online payment via
  Razorpay) and payment status
- **Delivery status and timestamps** (accepted, prepared, picked up,
  delivered)
- **Shop and delivery partner identifiers** associated with your
  order

### 2.4 Device and technical information

We collect:

- **Approximate location** — used only to show you nearby shops. If
  you decline location access, the App falls back to a default
  location and continues to work.
- **Device push notification token** — used to send you order status
  updates (when your order is accepted, ready, out for delivery, and
  delivered). Stored on our servers and removed when you delete the
  App.
- **Crash and performance data** — anonymized, used to fix bugs.

### 2.5 What we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect or store your payment card or bank account
  details. All payment information is collected directly by our
  payment processor (Razorpay) and is never sent to or stored by us.
- We do **not** collect biometric data (Face ID, fingerprint).
- We do **not** access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
- We do **not** track your activity across other apps or websites.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

- Authenticate your identity via phone OTP
- Show you nearby shops and their menus
- Process your orders and route them to the appropriate shop and
  delivery partner
- Send you order status notifications
- Provide customer support if you contact us
- Diagnose technical problems and improve the App
- Comply with legal obligations

We do **not** use your information for advertising, marketing
campaigns, or sale to third parties.

---

## 4. Who we share your information with

We share information only with the service providers necessary to
operate the App. These providers are contractually bound to use the
information only for the services they provide to us.

| Service provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Google (Firebase) | Authentication, database, cloud functions, file storage | Account, profile, order, device data |
| Razorpay | Payment processing | Order amount, your name and phone for payment receipts |
| Expo (Expo Application Services) | Push notification delivery | Your device push token and notification content |
| Sentry | Crash reporting and error monitoring | Anonymized crash data, device model, OS version |

We may share information with:

- **Shop owners** — your name, phone, and delivery address for
  orders you place at their shop, so they can prepare and arrange
  delivery
- **Delivery partners** — your name, phone, and delivery address for
  orders assigned to them, so they can complete delivery. The phone
  number is only shown after they accept your order
- **Government authorities** — when required by law or legal process
  in India

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties under
any circumstances.

---

## 5. Data retention

- **Account data** is retained while your account is active. You may
  request deletion at any time by emailing
  sarastacklabs@gmail.com.
- **Order history** is retained for at least 3 years after the order
  date, for tax and regulatory reasons under Indian law.
- **Saved addresses** are retained until you delete them.
- **Anonymized crash/performance data** is retained for up to 90
  days.

---

## 6. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and
similar regulations, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate information (via the Profile screen, or by
  contacting us)
- **Delete** your account and associated personal information (we
  will retain order history as required by law, but anonymize it)
- **Withdraw consent** for any optional data collection (e.g.
  location). Withdrawing consent may limit some App functionality

To exercise these rights, email **sarastacklabs@gmail.com**. We will
respond within 30 days.

---

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your
information, including:

- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS / TLS)
- Server-side authentication and authorization checks
- Restricted access to personal data by our team and service
  providers

No system is perfectly secure. We will notify you promptly if we
become aware of a data breach that affects your personal
information.

---

## 8. Children's privacy

HamaraSetu is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do
not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe we have collected such information, please contact
us at sarastacklabs@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.
