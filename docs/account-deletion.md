# Account Deletion — HamaraSetu

**App:** HamaraSetu (हमारा सेतु)
**Operating entity:** Sara Stack Labs, Faridabad, Haryana, India
**Contact:** sudhir.davim@gmail.com

This page describes how to request deletion of your HamaraSetu
account and the data associated with it.

---

## How to request account deletion

Send an email to **sudhir.davim@gmail.com** with:

- **Subject:** `Account deletion request — HamaraSetu`
- **From:** the email address you have on file with us (if any), or
  any email address you can be reached at
- **Body:** include the **phone number** you used to register on
  HamaraSetu (this is how we identify your account, since the app
  uses phone-OTP login)

We will reply within **5 business days** to acknowledge your request
and may ask you to verify your identity by responding to an OTP sent
to your registered phone number, before we proceed with deletion.

Once your identity is verified, your account and the data described
below will be deleted within **30 days**.

---

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently
removed from our systems:

- Your profile (name, phone number, email if provided)
- All saved delivery addresses associated with your account
- Your favorites list (saved items at each shop)
- Push-notification tokens for your devices
- Your authored ratings and review comments (the ratings continue to
  influence the shop's aggregate rating but are anonymised — the
  ratings can no longer be traced back to you)
- If you are a shop owner: your KYC documents (Identity Proof, GST
  Certificate, Shop Registration, storefront photo) are deleted from
  Firebase Storage; your shop is marked inactive and removed from
  customer listings
- If you are a delivery partner: your reported location data and
  online-status history

---

## What is retained and why

Some data is retained after account deletion, for legal, tax, or
fraud-prevention reasons. The retained data is **disassociated from
your personal identity** wherever possible.

| Data | Why retained | Retention period |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Order records (order ID, items, amount, date) | Indian Income Tax Act and GST law require businesses to retain transaction records | **7 years** from the date of the transaction, after which they are permanently deleted |
| Payment-processor records (held by Razorpay) | Required by Razorpay's terms and applicable Indian payment regulations; held by Razorpay, not by us | Per Razorpay's data retention policy |
| Audit logs of admin actions taken on your account | Fraud prevention, dispute resolution, regulatory compliance | **3 years** |

Retained records are not used for any purpose other than the legal /
regulatory ones listed above.

---

## Deleting individual data items without deleting your account

We currently offer account-level deletion only. To remove individual
items (e.g., a specific saved address or a specific past order)
without deleting your entire account, please contact us at
**sudhir.davim@gmail.com** with the specific request and we will
process it on a best-effort basis within the same 30-day window.
